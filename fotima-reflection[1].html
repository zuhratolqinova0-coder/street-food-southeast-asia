Reflection

This project taught me to move beyond describing the positive cultural value of street food and to examine the tensions behind it. I learned that street food can support livelihoods and preserve traditions, while vendors may still face unstable income, unequal regulation, and limited infrastructure. I also learned that research gaps are important because they show which groups or experiences have received less attention. I will apply critical analysis by asking whose perspectives are included, who benefits, and what evidence supports a claim.
