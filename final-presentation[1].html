Presentation

The final recorded presentation must be supplied by the team. This page currently provides planning content, not a completed recording.

Presentation overview

The recorded presentation should summarize the website's argument in 15 minutes. All team members must participate. The presentation should not read every website paragraph; it should communicate the central question, strongest evidence, key insights, and future recommendations.

Minutes 1–5: Project overview

Introduce the team and the topic. Define street food and explain why it matters to food, culture, language, identity, economy, politics, and lifestyle. Present the central research question and explain the five selected country cases.

Minutes 6–10: Knowledge base and contributions

Present ten key insights: (1) street food reflects historical exchange; (2) food names carry cultural meaning; (3) street food can strengthen community interaction; (4) vendors support local livelihoods; (5) informal businesses face economic vulnerability; (6) regulation affects access to public space; (7) food safety depends on infrastructure as well as individual practice; (8) tourism creates opportunities and pressures; (9) digital platforms change customer relationships; and (10) cultural preservation must include vendors and their knowledge.

Minutes 11–15: Transformative vision and future research

Present recommendations for inclusive regulation, access to clean water and sanitation, affordable vendor training, responsible tourism, and community-led documentation of food traditions. Identify research gaps involving gender, migration, digital platforms, sustainability, and intergenerational knowledge. End with the project's central conclusion.

Presentation quality checklist

Use readable slides, consistent typography, limited text, clear visuals, and APA in-text citations. Ensure that all team members speak. Add a final references slide even though it is optional, because it strengthens academic transparency. Check audio, video, timing, and playback before submission.
