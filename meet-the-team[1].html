Meet The Team

Our team

This project is a collaborative digital portfolio created by twelve students. Each member contributes a different research or production responsibility. The team combines historical, sociocultural, economic, political, linguistic, globalization, critical, editorial, and presentation perspectives.

How we work

Team members develop their assigned sections, identify credible sources, and provide evidence for their claims. The research coordinator checks source relevance and citation details. The editor reviews academic English and consistency. The website coordinator organizes the material into a clear and accessible digital portfolio. The team leader and project coordinator support communication and completion of the project.

Team photographs and short biographies should be added only after each member provides or approves their own photo and biography.
